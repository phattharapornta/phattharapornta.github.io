Privacy Policy: Portfolio Tracker

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Who uses it

Portfolio Tracker is a private app used only by its owner, for the owner's own portfolio. It has no other users.

What it accesses

  • Google Sheets: reads and updates the owner's own portfolio spreadsheet (prices, holdings and transactions).
  • Gmail (read-only): reads the owner's own emails from brokers and fund managers to find trade confirmations. It never sends, changes or deletes email.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to keep the owner's portfolio up to date. It stays in the owner's own Google account and in the owner's own private storage. It is not sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
